# Decisions

Record major product and architecture decisions here using short ADR-style notes.

Suggested filename format:

```text
0001-short-decision-title.md
```

Suggested structure:

```markdown
# Decision Title

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded

## Context

What problem or tradeoff forced the decision?

## Decision

What choice was made?

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or intentionally deferred?
```
